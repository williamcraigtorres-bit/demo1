Career Story

Maradona made his professional debut for Argentinos Juniors at only 15. His close control, balance, imagination, and fearless personality quickly made him a national sensation. He later joined Boca Juniors and helped his childhood club win the Argentine league.

After moving to Europe, he won three Spanish trophies with Barcelona. His greatest club chapter came at Napoli, where he inspired the club to its first two Serie A championships as well as the Coppa Italia, UEFA Cup, and Italian Super Cup.

Maradona's defining international achievement was the 1986 World Cup. As Argentina's captain, he scored five goals, supplied five assists, and produced one of the most influential individual tournaments in football history.

Why He Ranks #3 All Time

Maradona's 1986 World Cup remains one of the greatest individual tournament performances ever. His imagination, courage, personality, and ability to decide matches made him completely unique.

He also changed Napoli forever, turning an unfancied club into champions. At his peak, very few players have ever controlled a match or inspired a team as powerfully as Diego Maradona.
