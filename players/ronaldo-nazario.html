Career Story

Ronaldo began his senior career at Cruzeiro, where his speed, strength, and finishing immediately stood out. He moved to PSV while still a teenager and scored at a remarkable rate before joining Barcelona.

His single season at Barcelona was one of the most explosive campaigns ever produced by a striker. At Inter Milan he became a global superstar, although serious knee injuries threatened to end his career during his prime.

Ronaldo completed one of football's greatest comebacks at the 2002 World Cup. He scored eight goals, including both goals in the final, before joining Real Madrid and becoming one of the leading figures of the Galacticos era.

Why He Ranks #4 All Time

At his peak, Ronaldo Nazario combined a sprinter's acceleration, a dribbler's control, and an elite striker's finishing. Defenders could not prepare for his mixture of speed, strength, imagination, and calmness.

His comeback to lead Brazil to the 2002 World Cup made his story even more extraordinary. Injuries shortened his absolute prime, but O Fenômeno still produced a level of attacking brilliance that very few players have matched.
