Career Story

Cristiano Ronaldo began his professional career with Sporting CP in Portugal. His speed, dribbling and confidence quickly attracted the attention of Manchester United and Sir Alex Ferguson.

At Manchester United, Ronaldo developed from an exciting winger into one of the world's most dangerous goalscorers. He won three Premier League titles, the Champions League and his first Ballon d'Or.

Ronaldo joined Real Madrid in 2009 and became the club's all-time leading goalscorer. He scored 450 competitive goals in 438 appearances while winning four more Champions League titles with the club.

He later won trophies with Juventus, returned to Manchester United and continued his career with Al-Nassr. For Portugal, he helped win UEFA Euro 2016 and two UEFA Nations League titles.

Why He Ranks #2 All Time

Cristiano Ronaldo combines incredible goalscoring, longevity, athleticism and a legendary winning mentality. He succeeded in England, Spain, Italy, Europe and international football while breaking records that may stand for generations.

His ability to continuously reinvent himself—from a skillful winger into one of football's greatest goalscorers—is what makes him my second-greatest player of all time.
