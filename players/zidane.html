Career Story

Zidane made his professional debut with Cannes at 17 before moving to Bordeaux, where his touch, balance, and passing made him one of France's most admired young midfielders. He helped Bordeaux win the Intertoto Cup and reach the 1996 UEFA Cup final.

At Juventus, Zidane became a world-class playmaker. He won two Serie A championships and several international trophies before earning the Ballon d'Or in 1998, the same year he led France to its first World Cup.

Zidane joined Real Madrid in 2001 and completed his collection with the Champions League. His winning volley in the 2002 final remains one of the competition's greatest goals, perfectly capturing his technique and calmness.

Why He Ranks #6 All Time

Zidane made the most difficult parts of football look calm and effortless. His control, awareness, physical balance, and passing allowed him to command matches without rushing.

More importantly, he repeatedly produced defining moments in World Cup, European Championship, and Champions League matches. Few midfielders have combined beauty, authority, and big-game influence as completely as Zizou.
